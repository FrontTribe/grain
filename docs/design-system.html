Design System · v0.1

Grain design system

The visual language of a provenance tool. Its whole thesis is a duotone: human and AI as two materials in one grain. Everything derives from that.

00 Foundation

Grain doesn't borrow the default dev-tool look (near-black + acid green, or a purple gradient hero). It reads like a precise engineering ledger printed on warm stock: measured, material, trustworthy. The duotone is not decoration — it is the data.

material 01

Human

Deep viridian. Steady, crafted, owned. The default and the ground truth.

material 02

AI-assisted

Burnt sienna. Warm, generated, fast. Never framed as "bad" — just different grain.

The mark is a rounded square with a human→AI gradient — a "seed" from which the grain grows. Wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque 800, lowercase, tight tracking:  grain

01 Color

Neutrals are warm putty, not grey — chosen, biased slightly toward the accent. Two semantic hues (human / AI) carry meaning and must never be swapped for decoration. Every token is defined for both themes; swatches below render in the current theme, hexes list both.

Color rule
--human and --ai encode meaning. Do not use them as generic accents, and do not introduce a third data hue. "Unclassified" uses --line-strong, deliberately colorless.

02 Typography

Three roles, three faces. A characterful grotesque for display, a humanist sans for reading, and a mono for everything that is data — badges, tokens, labels, code. The mono is not just for code; it is the "instrument readout" voice of the product.

Display · Bricolage Grotesque · 700–800
See the grain.
Headlines, wordmark, big numbers. Tight tracking (-.02 to -.03em), weight 800 for hero, 700 for sections.
Body · IBM Plex Sans · 400–600
Provenance, measured honestly — with a confidence level on every claim.
Running text at ~16px, 65ch measure. 500/600 for emphasis and UI labels.
Data · IBM Plex Mono · 400–600

Badges, chips, table headers, code, %s, SHAs, eyebrows. Letter-spacing .06–.16em when uppercased.

03 The grain motif

The signature graphic: a commit history rendered as a barcode of thin bars, each colored by authorship. It is grain's equivalent of a fingerprint — instantly recognizable, and it doubles as real data viz. A simpler stacked bar (the "provenance bar") is its compact form.

04 Space & shape

A 4px base grid. Radii step by role — small for chips, medium for buttons, larger for cards, full for pills. Elevation is restrained: one soft shadow token, used only on the hero fingerprint card and floating surfaces.

06 Voice & tone

For a provenance tool, copy is part of the design system — the wrong word turns a signal into an accusation. The voice is a calm instrument reading a measurement: precise, probabilistic, never moralizing.

PrincipleSayNever
Signals, not verdicts"62% of lines carry AI signals""this PR is AI-written / faked"
Own the uncertainty"inferred · confidence 0.6""detected AI code"
Neutral about AI"AI-assisted""contaminated / suspicious"
Actionable, calm"1 human review requested""BLOCKED — violation"
People, not policing"transparency for maintainers""catch developers cheating"

07 Do & don't

Do

  • Keep human = viridian, AI = sienna, everywhere. Consistency is comprehension.
  • Use mono for any number, %, SHA, or label — it is the instrument voice.
  • Let the warm putty ground breathe; whitespace is a feature.
  • Reserve the shadow + fingerprint for one hero moment per view.
  • Pair every inferred claim with its confidence.

Don't

  • Don't use red/green as pass/fail — AI is not "fail". Sienna ≠ error.
  • Don't add a third data hue or a gradient background hero.
  • Don't center everything or scatter emoji as section markers.
  • Don't state an inferred result as fact — no naked "AI detected".
  • Don't let the mark's gradient leak into UI fills; it is for the seed only.

:root {
